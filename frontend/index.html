Process

Four Steps to Placement

A structured, transparent workflow from registration to confirmed offer.

01 / REGISTER
Create Your Profile
Students register with USN, branch, CGPA, and skills. Companies sign up with hiring requirements and company info.
02 / DISCOVER
Browse Opportunities
Live drives filtered automatically by CGPA eligibility. Students only see roles they qualify for — no manual filtering needed.
03 / APPLY
Apply in One Click
Smart eligibility check before submission. Status updates in real time — Applied, Shortlisted, Selected, or Rejected with remarks.
04 / PLACED
Get Placed
Companies move candidates through the pipeline. Admin monitors the entire placement season with live analytics and full control.

Who It Serves

One Platform, Three Stakeholders

Dedicated portals built for each user type with exactly the tools they need.

For Students
Build your profile, discover eligible drives, apply instantly, and track every application through the recruitment pipeline in real time.
  • CGPA-based job matching
  • One-click application
  • Live status updates with remarks
  • Profile and resume management
  • Application withdrawal support
For Companies
Post structured placement drives, manage applicants, and update candidate status with remarks directly from the company dashboard.
  • Post jobs with eligibility criteria
  • Filter applicants by CGPA
  • Update status with remarks
  • Access student resumes
  • Open and close drives anytime
For Placement Officers
Full administrative control over the entire placement lifecycle with platform-wide analytics, oversight tools, and student management.
  • Platform-wide live analytics
  • Manage all students and companies
  • Override any application status
  • Monitor all active drives
  • Placement rate reporting
